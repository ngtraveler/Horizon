# Marine and yachting profile

Use this profile for items about the leisure marine industry and the supply chain that serves it.

In scope:

- Yachts and superyachts: new builds, deliveries, launches, order books, refits, shipyards, naval architects and designers, brokers, charter and management companies.
- Sailing: racing and cruising, one-design and rating classes, major events (America's Cup, Vendée Globe, The Ocean Race, Olympic sailing, classic regattas), seamanship and safety.
- Marine equipment and marine electronics: engines, sterndrives, jets, electric and hybrid propulsion, batteries and charging, navigation and chartplotters, AIS, radar, autopilots, sonar, sensors, satellite and cellular connectivity, audio, stabilisers, thrusters, deck hardware, sails and rigging.
- Trade and distribution: boat shows (METSTRADE, boot Düsseldorf, Cannes, Monaco, Fort Lauderdale), dealers and distributors, aftermarket, pricing, inventory, recalls.
- Commercial maritime context that a builder, equipment vendor, marina, or boat owner would actually track: certification and regulation (CE/RCD, ABYC, USCG safety alerts, IMO and emissions rules), port and shipyard capacity, safety investigations that change design or operation, and shipping economics when they affect this industry.

Out of scope:

- General technology, AI, or software news with no marine application.
- General sports results, celebrity lifestyle, travel features, or property pieces where a boat is only scenery.
- Commercial shipping topics with no bearing on the leisure marine industry (container spot rates, bulk commodities, crewing shortages) unless the item explicitly connects to builders, equipment, regulation, or boat owners.
- Pure promotion: giveaways, sponsored listicles, award nominations with no product specifics, or press releases that restate marketing copy.
